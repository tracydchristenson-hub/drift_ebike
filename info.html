OUR VALUES

Quality. Design. Performance.

Drift E-Bike is a new e-bike brand centered around quality, design and performance. We make electric bikes for those who value a premium product without the tech bro energy.

New to e-bikes? No problem! We’re happy to help you with the basics.

Like regular bikes, riding an e-bike is good exercise. The difference is that the motor on an electric bike allows the rider to travel faster than they could on a regular bike, up to 20 to 28 mph. The boost in speed allows the rider to travel longer distances in less time, making e-bikes a good choice for regular commutes that save the user money for fuel and parking. They’re great for the environment because they produce no emissions! The power boost an e-bike provides also saves the user some physical effort and allows the rider to travel at a more constant speed, even going up and down hills.

You don’t need to be a gearhead or a tech whiz to own and ride an e-bike, but we have some helpful information about the parts of an e-bike and its maintenance.

The Frame

Our frames are built from premium aluminum and carbon fiber. Aluminum frames are durable and easy to repair, and they cost less than their carbon counterparts. The advantage of carbon fiber frames is that they are lighter than aluminum, reducing the overall weight of the e-bike, but they do cost more.

The Motor

E-bike motors vary in power from 250W to 750W. When you order your e-bike, you can customize the motor to suit your riding habits. Motors with higher power are useful for riders who expect to ride uphill or over rough terrain, and they also allow the bike to travel faster. However, higher wattage motors increase the weight of the bike and use more battery power.

The Battery

Battery size and power vary, and we allow customization. We recommend basing your battery choice on the distance you plan to travel. A larger battery allows the rider to travel longer distances, but it will also cost more and make the bike heavier. The distance an e-bike can travel before the battery needs to be charged depends on the bike’s specifications and the terrain. Most e-bikes can travel about 25 to 50 miles on average before a battery charge, with rough conditions taking more battery power. Most batteries recharge to 100% in about 3 to 6 hours. The lifespan of a battery is about 3 to 6 years or 500 to 1,000 charge cycles.

Pedal Assist and Throttle

The pedal assist regulates the power that the motor provides proportional to the power of the rider’s pedaling. This feature is required by law and helps the rider anticipate how their own physical exertion will affect their ride. Not all e-bikes use throttles, but they allow the bike to operate on power from the motor without the rider pedaling the bike. Throttles on e-bikes can be either hand-activated or foot-activated.

The Brakes

Our e-bikes use hydraulic brakes so you can stop safely, even at fast speeds or over rough terrain.

FAQs

How do I choose which model of e-bike is best for me?

Which model you choose depends on your personal riding habits. Drift Electric Bike Company offers bikes that are great for commuters, casual city riders, or people who like to ride off-road. When choosing your e-bike, it’s important to consider where you plan to ride it. We also offer customization options to make your bike right for you!

Do I need a driver’s license or an operator’s license to ride an e-bike?

Local regulations vary, but most U.S. states don’t require a license for an e-bike rider. They also don’t typically require the e-bike to be registered or for the rider to have insurance.

How do you clean an e-bike?

You can use water, with or without a bike shampoo from a specialty shop. The electric parts of the bike are water resistant, but you should always remove the battery before washing the bike. Never submerge the bike or use any form of power wash.
